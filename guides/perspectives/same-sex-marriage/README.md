# Same-Sex Marriage and Christian Faithfulness: A Six-Perspective Study

*A structured, adversarial examination of the question: **can a Christian bless a same-sex marriage and remain faithful?***

## How This Was Built

Six independent perspectives were developed, each assigned a fixed position and a fixed vantage point, and each instructed to argue its case at full strength while conceding the genuine weaknesses of its own view in a required closing section.

|  | Affirming (yes, it can be faithful) | Traditional (no, it is not faithful) |
|---|---|---|
| **Literary / biblical scholar** — text, language, genre, and original cultural context only; no outside opinion | [01](01-affirming-literary-scholar.md) | [04](04-traditional-literary-scholar.md) |
| **Pastor** — leading and teaching a real congregation through the issue | [02](02-affirming-pastor.md) | [05](05-traditional-pastor.md) |
| **Non-believer** — secular outside analyst, no religious commitment | [03](03-affirming-secular-analyst.md) | [06](06-traditional-secular-analyst.md) |

## Ground Rules Imposed on Every Perspective

1. **Argue the strongest version.** No strawmen. Where the other side has scored a real point, concede it.
2. **Stay in your lane.** The scholars argue from text and context alone. The pastors argue pastoral theology and church practice. The secular analysts argue from history, hermeneutics as a general discipline, and evidence.
3. **Name your weaknesses.** Every document ends with a section stating the strongest objections to its own position.
4. **Treat people as people.** The question is about texts, communities, and practices — never a license to disparage anyone.

## A Note on Status

These documents are an aid to thinking, not a ruling. The question has been argued in good faith by serious Christians for decades, and the disagreement runs between people who share the same Scriptures and the same commitment to obeying them. Nothing here is a substitute for a reader's own study, their community, and their conscience.
